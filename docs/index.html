How the picks are made

Pressure, not goals

Every team carries home and away attacking and defensive ratings in shots plus corners. They update after each match; goals are too noisy to predict from.

Nudging the market

The chance of over 2.5 comes from a logistic regression on the match pressure and the market's own price, so the model only moves the market a little.

Paper's rules

No picks until both teams have played six league games, and none in either team's last six. Ratings refresh with each round of results.

Small edge, real variance

The paper made about 0.8% per bet at the best bookmaker prices and lost at average prices. Treat the record here as the real test.
